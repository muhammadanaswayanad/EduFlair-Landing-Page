Why Choose Ausbildung in Germany?

A free German education pathway combining theoretical learning and practical training with a monthly stipend.

Earn While You Learn

Receive a monthly stipend of €1,200–€1,700 during your training period.

No Tuition Fees

Ausbildung is fully funded by the employer, making it incredibly cost-effective.

Job Security

Over 90% of students receive full-time job offers immediately after completing the program.

PR Pathway

Ausbildung graduates can work in Germany and apply for permanent residency (PR) after 2 years.

High Starting Salary

Graduates earn a competitive salary starting from €2,800–€3,500 per month, with high growth opportunities.

No Blocked Account

Unlike university education, Ausbildung does not require a blocked account, saving you immense upfront costs.
