Why is a stipend provided during Ausbildung?

Ausbildung combines theory with hands-on practical training, meaning students work as part of their training. Since they contribute to the workforce, they receive a monthly stipend ranging from €1,000 to €1,400 (approximately ₹1 Lakh), covering their living expenses in Germany.

What is the stay-back period after completing Ausbildung?

A stay-back period is not required. Upon successful completion, students can immediately start working in Germany. Most graduates receive job offers from the same employer who trained them.

Is there any job contract or bond?

No, there is no mandatory bond or contract. However, for professional growth and stability, it is recommended that students stay with their employer for a few years before switching jobs.

Is a blocked account required for Ausbildung?

No, Ausbildung does not require a blocked account or show money, unlike university education. The monthly stipend is sufficient to cover living costs, making it a financially viable option.


Can I pursue further studies after completing Ausbildung?

Yes, Germany offers tuition-free higher education, and Ausbildung graduates can pursue related degrees or other advanced programs, provided they meet the eligibility criteria.

Are only Goethe certifications accepted for admission?

No, it depends on the employer. While Goethe is widely accepted, other recognized German language certifications like ECL, OSD, and TELC may also be accepted.

How long does the Ausbildung process take?

- German Language Learning: Minimum 8 months (up to B2 level).
- Exam Booking & Passing: Can take additional time based on availability and results.
- Application & Processing: Around 6 months after clearing the required language certification.
- Visa Processing: Dependent on the German embassy.
- Total Timeline: Expect around 1.5 years from the start of language training to reaching Germany.

What are the eligibility requirements for Ausbildung?

- Minimum 55% in 12th grade.
- B2 level German certification..
- Age limit: Ideally between 18-25
years (can vary based on the employer).

Can I switch employers during Ausbildung?

Switching is possible but not recommended, as it may delay graduation or impact work experience. It’s best to complete training with one employer for smoother career progression.

What happens if I fail my German language exam?

You will need to reappear for the exam. This may delay your intake, so it’s important to stay consistent in your preparation and aim to clear the exam as early as possible.
